Evaluation scope & protocol

Resimulation: Ep0 starts 189, 225 and 241. Generalization: Ep1 starts 181 and 260. Each rollout is initialized once from the starting observation and predicts 15 consecutive frames. The figures are selected examples; the tables contain every agreed window.

Both models were trained on Ep0, with different training budgets and conditioning. G5 uses recorded action and tactile conditions; SoMA uses destination controller positions without tactile input. SoMA's original real_dt = 1/15 s and gravity scale 4 are retained, while the observed frame interval is approximately 1/30 s. This is a comparison of the existing systems, not a matched-budget architecture ablation.

“Generalization” refers to selected strong-motion windows in a new episode of the same cloth, not a new object or a complete test set. RGB/Alpha targets are rendered GT Gaussians (SH degree 0, black background), not camera photographs.
